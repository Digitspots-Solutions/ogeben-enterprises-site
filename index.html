About Us

Ogeben Enterprises is a leading manufacturing company based in Obio Akpor, Port Harcourt. Located at No 2 KINIKA Road, Ogborogbo Town, we have built our reputation on delivering superior manufactured products that meet the highest industry standards.

Our state-of-the-art facility combines modern manufacturing techniques with skilled craftsmanship to produce reliable, durable products for our valued clients across Nigeria. We are committed to quality control, timely delivery, and exceptional customer service in every project we undertake.
