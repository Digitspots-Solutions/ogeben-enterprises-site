About Us

Jubilee Oven Confectionery Limited (also known as Jubilee Oven Sweet Treats Co) is a leading FoodTech confectionery business based in Obio Akpor, Port Harcourt. Operated by experienced restaurant technology professionals, we combine cutting-edge food preparation techniques with time-honored baking traditions to create exceptional sweet treats that delight our customers.

From our location at No 2 KINIKA Road, Ogborogbo Town, we serve Port Harcourt and surrounding communities with premium cakes, pastries, bread, and confectioneries made fresh daily. Our commitment to quality ingredients, innovative recipes, and customer satisfaction has made us a trusted name in Rivers State's thriving food scene.

Whether you're celebrating a special occasion, hosting an event, or simply treating yourself, Jubilee Oven delivers sweetness with every bite.
